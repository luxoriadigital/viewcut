    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    How does the free 30-second test work?

    Send your footage and the clip you care about: I send back those 30 seconds edited, subtitled and watermarked — free. If you like the result, we talk about the rest; if not, you keep the 30 seconds.

    Is it a tool or a service?

    A service. You don't open anything, you don't set anything up: there's a request, an edit, and an approval before delivery. The machine speeds up production, it doesn't decide in your place.

    How do I send my footage?

    A Drive, WeTransfer or Dropbox link, plus one line on what the video should do. No meeting to schedule.

    What exactly do I get?

    One MP4 per format (9:16, 16:9, 1:1), burned-in subtitles, and a timestamped chapter list, ready to paste under your video.

    What's the turnaround?

    The turnaround is stated before any payment; a short video usually goes out the same day, and steady volume gets a reserved slot.

    Do you work with agencies?

    Yes, as a white-label partner: your deliverables, your name, my editing behind the scenes.
